Our Heritage

History of Shree Durga Devi Temple

Shree Durga Devi Temple, located at Byadageri Oni, Hubballi, has been a sacred place of worship for many years. The temple is a symbol of faith, unity, and devotion, bringing together devotees from the local community during festivals, poojas, and religious celebrations.

With the blessings of Goddess Durga Devi and the support of devotees, the temple committee is now rebuilding and renovating the temple to preserve its spiritual heritage for future generations.

About Our Temple

Shree Durga Devi Temple, Byadageri Oni, Hubballi, is a sacred place of worship where devotees gather with faith and devotion. The temple has been serving the community for many years by celebrating religious festivals, cultural events, and social activities.

Our committee is currently rebuilding the temple to create a better spiritual place for future generations. Every contribution and blessing helps us move closer to this goal.
